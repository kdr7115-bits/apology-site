Dear Babu, 💕

I am genuinely and so sorry for every point where I hurt you or tried to control you. 😔 I’m sorry that I sometimes didn’t show concern for you, and that I didn’t respect you, or even disrespected you, in many ways. If you think I’m becoming the weaker one, please give me some strength. Even a small bit of your strength is enough for me. And if I ever feel that you are losing strength or you need me, I am always there for you. 🫂 We will work it out completely and make this the most, most, most lovely bond ever. 💞

I’m not writing this to impress you. Genuinely, this is the last edge of my creativity, the one way I can apologize in my own way. So please, please accept it. 🙏 I know I have said sorry thousands of times, and still I have repeated some of my mistakes. I feel that, and I truly accept my mistakes. But you matter to me the most. Even when I make mistakes or get angry, even after any of our small, cute nok-jhok, my heart forces me to choose you again and again. 🥹💗
